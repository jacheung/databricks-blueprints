Benchmark pipeline — Delta vs Lance, one engine

One deterministic Spark source, three storage formats. All data engineering — generate, write, backfill — runs on pure Spark, so the only variable is the terminal format. 02_run_benchmarks submits each (format × size) as a parallel job on its own fresh 8-worker Spark cluster — so no route reuses another's cache. Fixed seed & cluster.
